Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Manyoni Ridge Lodge Operations System and WhatsApp bot ("the System"), operated by Manyoni Ridge Safari Lodge.

Information We Collect

The System collects information provided by staff members including:

  • Name, phone number, and employment details
  • Operational data such as fuel logs, stock movements, and task updates
  • Environmental observations including wildlife sightings and field reports
  • WhatsApp messages sent to the operations bot

How We Use Information

Information collected is used exclusively for internal lodge operations management including:

  • Staff management and HR administration
  • Operational reporting and cost tracking
  • Conservation and environmental monitoring
  • Safety and compliance management

Data Storage

All data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL) servers. Data is not sold or shared with third parties outside of lodge operations.

WhatsApp Integration

The System uses the WhatsApp Business API to receive operational reports from registered staff members. Only staff members with registered phone numbers can interact with the bot. All WhatsApp interactions are logged for operational and compliance purposes.

Data Retention

Operational data is retained for a minimum of 7 years for compliance purposes. Staff personal data is retained for the duration of employment and 5 years thereafter.

Your Rights

Staff members may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting lodge management directly.
